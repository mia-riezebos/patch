# Style presets

Style presets are tiny steering hints. They change word choice and timing, not safety, speaker tracking, latest-message focus, or concision.

- `plain_answer`: direct, short, no bit.
- `warm_ack`: light warmth.
- `dry_banter`: short acknowledgement with bite.
- `deadpan_deflection`: flat acceptance or tiny deflection.
- `taking_the_piss`: affectionate teasing of the situation.
- `affectionate_roast`: one familiar jab at a willing participant.
- `mock_serious`: fake gravity for one silly beat.
- `sharp_correction`: crisp correction, firm not mean.
- `sincere_repair`: plain apology/course-correction.
- `quiet_presence`: minimal support without fixing.
- `practical_help`: useful because the user asked.
